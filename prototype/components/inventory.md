# Inventory

## Core Idea

Tiny equipment system. Supports combat without becoming main game.

## Slots

- Weapon: 1 item
- Armor: 1 item
- Consumable: 1 item stack

## Starting Loadout

- Wooden Sword
- Cloth Armor
- Apple x3

## Verbs

- Equip
- Consume
- Display

## Rules

- Weapon modifies attack.
- Armor reduces combat damage.
- Apple heals Combat HP only.
- Apple cannot heal Life.
- Consumable cannot be used at full Combat HP.
- Consumable cannot be used when count is 0.
- No backpack/grid in prototype.

## UI

HUD shows:
- weapon name/icon
- armor name/icon
- consumable name/icon/count

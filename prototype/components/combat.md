# Combat

## Core Idea

Simple starter combat for field engagement. Separate from Life economy.

## Actors

- Player
- Slime
- Bat
- Rat

## Resources

- Combat HP
- Enemy HP
- Attack
- Defense
- XP

## Verbs

- Fight
- Attack
- Take Damage
- Defeat
- Gain XP
- Consume Apple

## Rules

- Combat damage affects Combat HP only.
- Combat never spends Life.
- Enemy defeat grants XP.
- Weapon adds attack.
- Armor reduces incoming damage.
- Apple heals Combat HP.

## Prototype Enemies

- Slime: tanky/simple tutorial
- Bat: quick aerial starter
- Rat: fast/medium ground starter

## Open Decision

- What happens when Combat HP reaches 0? Return to Town, respawn in Field, or lose XP TBD.
